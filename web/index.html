02Problem & AWS team alignment

Middle-mile fulfillment networks and LEO satellite meshes share three coupled failure modes: static shortest-path plans keep dispatching freight or packets onto blizzard-closed hubs and rain-faded Ka-band links (< 8.0 dB SNR), at-least-once SQS metering retries overbill carrier/ISP accounts under binary floating-point math, and sequential daemon log scanning misdiagnoses cascading sympathetic alarms.

  • SCOT Shipment Optimization (ATROPS) & Supply Chain Automation: Successive augmenting min-cost paths reroute SEA1 → ORD5 → EWR7 volume across SLC2 → CVG3 Prime Air and DFW6 linehaul bypasses in < 1 ms while preserving Critical Pull Time (CPT) SLAs.
  • Amazon Leo Networking & Flight Software AI: Dynamic link-state pruning avoids attenuated Ka-band feeders and flapped optical laser ISLs, pairing deterministic ≤ 3-check predicate trees with grounded us.amazon.nova-lite-v1:0 flight diagnostics.
  • Financial & Inventory Conservation: Canonical SHA-256 idempotency_key deduplication inside SQLite BEGIN IMMEDIATE transactions enforces SUM(debits) == SUM(credits) with zero cent drift across multi-region tax schedules.
