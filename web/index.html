How It Works (30-Second Visual Walkthrough)

How RouteLedger keeps middle-mile parcel corridors and orbital laser backbones within SLA during severe weather while guaranteeing zero double-billing on at-least-once SQS settlement streams.

💡 Engineering Architecture in 3 Sentences: (1) Physical Rerouting: Static linehaul schedules and OSPF shortest-path tables blackhole freight and Gbps trunks when Chicago (ORD5) closes in a blizzard or Seattle Ka-band feeders drop below 8.0 dB SNR; RouteLedger runs successive shortest augmenting paths in <0.5 ms to split flow across residual air/ground and diversity orbital planes. (2) Bounded Triage: Instead of dumping noisy logs into an unconstrained prompt, an auditable ≤3-check decision tree isolates the exact hardware or weather root cause first and hands structured telemetry to AWS Bedrock (us.amazon.nova-lite-v1:0) for dispatch synthesis. (3) Financial Conservation: Every carrier and gateway usage event is settled inside SQLite BEGIN IMMEDIATE transactions with canonical SHA-256 idempotency seals and Decimal minor-unit tax rules across US-WA, DE, JP, and BR.
STEP 01 · SCOT SOLVER

Middle-Mile Min-Cost Flow

Models SEA1 → EWR7 across SLC2, DEN4, ORD5, DFW8, ATL6, and CVG3. Enforces hard 24.0h Critical Pull Time (CPT) windows and splits high-volume demand across Prime Air and southern ground bypasses when hubs degrade.

STEP 02 · AMAZON LEO MESH

Optical ISL & Rain-Fade Router

Continuously evaluates Ka-band feeder SNR (≥8.0 dB threshold) and 590 km inter-satellite laser links between Gateway-Redmond and Gateway-Frankfurt, diverting multi-Gbps trunks around rain attenuation and solar scintillation.

STEP 03 · NOC + BEDROCK

≤3-Check Triage + Nova Lite

Executes deterministic hardware vs. weather fault trees (100.0% accuracy across 150 incidents, 2.41 mean checks) before invoking live us.amazon.nova-lite-v1:0 on AWS Bedrock for concise operator runbooks.

STEP 04 · ACID LEDGER

SHA-256 Double-Entry Settlement

Deduplicates 60x at-least-once SQS retry storms to exact single postings, traps mutated payload replays via IdempotencyConflictError, and preserves SUM(Debits) == SUM(Credits) with zero IEEE-754 float drift.
